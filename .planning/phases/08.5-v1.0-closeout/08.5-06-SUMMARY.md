### Second-opinion findings the next planner must reach

All eight were independently re-verified against the working tree — by the
orchestrator before the ruling, and once more by the continuation agent while
recording it. Full text in the attribution record; condensed here.

1. **Extending the budget is wasted compute.** The guard-ordering defect is
   widespread (14 of 59 by the orchestrator's count; ~74 sites by Codex's
   broader audit), not a one-off.
2. **CORRECTION to an earlier reading in this summary and in the record.** The
   geometry site is **already ordered correctly**:
   `tests/test_wm_geometry.cpp:1490` carries
   `INFO("wm stderr: " << fixture.wmStderr())` **directly above** the assertion
   at `:1492`. This summary's "Issues Encountered" section below describes case
   \#93's guard as "a single guard covering only its own file's helper", which
   reads as though that site shares case \#80's ordering defect. **It does
   not.** Only the fallbacks site is mis-ordered — assertion at `:272`, guard at
   `:278`. The ordering defect is common but **not universal**, and the geometry
   site is a counter-example that a mechanical rewrite must not "fix".
3. **Hoisting the guard to the top of a case does NOT fix it.**
   `fixture.wmStderr()` is evaluated when the `INFO` statement executes, not when
   the annotated assertion fails, so a top-of-case guard captures stale
   pre-stall output. The correct shape binds the poll result first:
   `bool framed = pollUntil(...); INFO("wm stderr: " << fixture.wmStderr()); REQUIRE(framed);`
4. **The 8.23 s and 8.35 s figures are CENSORED OBSERVATIONS, not measured
   stalls.** They are the tests' own **8000 ms** reparent-poll deadlines expiring
   (`tests/test_wm_fallbacks.cpp:148`) plus fixture startup. The true stall
   length is unobserved and bounded only from below. This is why `longestms=0`
   does not contradict them — and it means the eight seconds **never supported
   the cold-cache hypothesis in the first place**.
5. **Do NOT split plan 08.5-07's fix.** Shipping the predicate narrowing (b)
   without the deadline (a) would make the hang **more** deterministic, not
   less: the foreign property events the wait currently consumes are what
   accidentally wake it. Verified sound against `src/Manager.cpp`. Relatedly,
   `foreign=5` of `cold=5` claims less than this summary's "Counter readings"
   section implies — "foreign" also covers the window manager's own root
   `_NET_CLIENT_LIST` and `_NET_ACTIVE_WINDOW` traffic, which
   `eventProperty()` (`src/Events.cpp:535`) would ignore anyway. The swallowing
   defect is real as a mechanism; that counter does not measure its impact.
6. **The causal chain is real and verified**, which is what keeps the hypothesis
   alive rather than refuted. The fixture readiness probe returns as soon as its
   own window is reparented (`tests/support/WmFixture.h:666`), but the window
   manager then proceeds `m_border->reparent()` (`src/Client.cpp:251`) →
   `activate()` (`src/Client.cpp:275`) → `timestamp()` (`src/Client.cpp:315`),
   where it can wedge. Construction can return "ready" while the window manager
   is about to block, and the **next** window's `MapRequest` is never processed.
